# Invented for this simulator, no real messages. Written before the first run of the
# rule in src/RankSim.kt and never edited after it: a change means new seeds.
#
# The topical app model. Each section is what one person writes about in one app, so
# the words a section repeats can hold places in that app's row beside everyday words.
# =name starts a section, @slot: a|b|c lists the entries {slot} may pick, and every
# other line is one message. How often a line or an entry comes up depends on the seed,
# as in templates.txt, and which sections the three apps get depends on the seed too.
